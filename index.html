COMMUNITY STORY

Connection should never be out of reach.

Gestalk is designed to help turn a gesture into a message that can be shared more easily. It supports the moments that make up daily life—from learning together to joining conversations with friends, family and teachers.

1Encourages more confident, independent communication.
2Creates a bridge between signed gestures and spoken or written language.
3Designed with a people-first, community-led purpose.
