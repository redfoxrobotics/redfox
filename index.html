GESTALK / SMART COMMUNICATION GLOVE

A small glove for more meaningful conversations.

Gestalk brings finger sensors and a wrist-mounted device together in one wearable. Use supported gestures to share a message through the companion app, with voice and text for the person you are speaking with.

Let's talk about Gestalk ↗
What does the Gestalk smart glove do?

Gestalk senses supported hand gestures and works with its companion app to present a message as voice, text and a visual cue. The app also supports converting a spoken reply into text.

Does Gestalk translate every sign?

Recognition depends on the supported gesture library and setup. Sign languages also use facial expressions, body movement and context. Ask our team which signs are available and how custom signs can be configured for your needs.

Who can explore a Gestalk demo?

Individuals, families, schools and organisations interested in accessible communication can contact Redfoxrobotics. We can walk you through the glove, app and supported conversation examples.

How can I get Gestalk?

Contact our team on WhatsApp or email to discuss availability, pricing, language support and a demonstration suited to your needs.
